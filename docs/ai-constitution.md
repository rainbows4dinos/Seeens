# Seeens: AI Constitution (draft)

Status: draft, November 1, 2026. Written to publish to users as a plain page, and to hold Seeens to.

## Why this exists

A lot of the DIY scene wants nothing to do with AI, and for good reasons: slop, art taken without consent, companies run by a few rich people, and data centers eating power and water. Seeens agrees with most of that.

Seeens is a directory. It helps bands keep track of who they know. A few tasks, like reading a flyer or looking up a band, can use AI to save typing. This page lists what Seeens will and won't do with it. If we break any of these, call us on it.

## The commitments

1. **Seeens never makes art.** No generated images, music, flyers, bios, or posts. Ever.
2. **Seeens never speaks for you.** It doesn't write your messages, and it doesn't send or post anything on your behalf.
3. **It's off unless your band turns it on.** AI features are disabled by default for every workspace. A band that never wants them never runs into them.
4. **You decide what's true.** Anything AI finds lands in a review inbox as a suggestion. Nothing is saved until a member accepts it.
5. **It shows its work.** Every suggestion comes with its source and the date of that source, so you can check it yourself.
6. **Your stuff is never training data.** Your connections, notes, and flyers aren't used to train any model, ours or anyone else's. We only use providers that commit to that in writing. We don't sell data, period.
7. **Plain tools first.** Where something simpler works, we use it: text recognition on your own device for flyers, or plain search links you open yourself. AI runs only when a member asks for it, never in the background or in bulk, and results are saved so the same lookup doesn't run twice.
8. **We say what runs where.** This page names every AI provider Seeens uses, what gets sent to it, and how long it's kept.
9. **It's never metered to you.** There are no tokens, credits, or AI upsells. Seeens' costs, AI included, come out of one shared pot, and the books are open.
10. **Changes happen in the open.** If any of this changes, we'll say so ahead of time, and your band can switch AI off before the change takes effect.

## What AI does in Seeens

When a band turns it on:

- **Read flyer:** pulls the lineup, venue, and date off a flyer or show post you upload.
- **Look up:** searches the web for a band or person you already track, to fill in links, members, and recent shows.
- **Find:** suggests bands, venues, and people in a scene or town that you might not know yet.

That's all.

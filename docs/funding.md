# Seeens: Funding and Ownership

Status: exploration, November 1, 2026. Post-MVP. Nothing here needs deciding before launch.

## Goal

Seeens isn't meant to turn a profit, but it has to cover its own costs to scale and last. No tiered subscription pricing. Ever.

## What it costs to run

- **Fixed and small:** Vercel, Supabase (likely the $25/month tier eventually), and the domain. A few hundred dollars a year covers many bands.
- **Variable:** opt-in AI features (Read flyer, Look up, Find). Costs scale with use, not with the number of bands. Capped per workspace.

## Comparable: Freakscene

[freakscene.diy](https://freakscene.diy/) is a DIY scene forum built on XenForo. As of November 1, 2026 its homepage listed about 20,600 members, 395 upcoming events, and 77 scenes, well beyond Portland.

- **Funding:** donations only. Its [support page](https://freakscene.diy/pages/support/) offers pay what you want with no perks, or one-time Stripe payments of $5 (supporter badge), $15 (adds stickers), and $40 (adds a tee).
- **How it relates to Seeens:** not really a competitor. Freakscene is where shows get announced and people hang out. Seeens is a band's private record of who it knows and what to do next. Freakscene event posts could be a capture source, the same as a flyer. Worth a friendly conversation at some point.

## Funding options

1. **Free core.** Directory, Homies, outreach, and CSV export are free forever.
2. **Pay what you want, with open books.** Publish monthly costs and income (Open Collective does this out of the box). "This month cost $41, bands chipped in $63."
3. **AI from the shared pot.** No tokens, credits, or metering. AI stays opt-in, on demand, cached, and capped per workspace so it fits the budget. (Per-use AI credits were considered and dropped: asking punk bands to buy AI is a non-starter.)
4. **Grants and fiscal sponsorship.** Local and state arts funders sometimes back community infrastructure. A fiscal sponsor allows tax-deductible donations without forming a nonprofit.
5. **Organizations pay, bands don't (maybe).** Small labels or collectives running several bands could pay a flat amount. Careful: charging venues or promoters for access to bands turns into pay-to-play.
6. **Merch.** Stickers and shirts are how this scene spreads things anyway. Candidate line: "Build your own fucking scene."

Starting point: 1, 2, and 3.

## Ruled out

- Tiered subscriptions.
- Ads.
- Selling or licensing scene data.
- Any cut of show revenue.
- Metered AI.

Saying so publicly is part of the pitch.

## Ownership and governance

Decided November 1, 2026:

- **No co-op for now.** Governance-first co-ops can spend more time on elections and discussion than on building (Subvert is the example Larry is watching).
- **One person decides product.** Larry builds and ships. No votes on features.
- **Exit is the real protection.** CSV export and open books keep Seeens honest more than a vote would.
- **Plain LLC with published commitments** until there's something worth owning.
- **Exit to community later, if Seeens grows to many bands.** Ownership would move to member bands, who would vote only on a short list: the AI Constitution, scene data use, and money.

## 2026-11-11

- **Home.** The first page is now your band’s own: your band at the top, what’s up next, your Homies and Let’s play as rows of photos, and what other bands just added near you. New members get Getting Started here too. Follow-ups lives here now, as Up Next, with a link to all of them.
- **One Directory.** Network and Explore are one page again, the Directory. It shows everyone, with Avoid at the very end. To see only what your band added or touched, pick **Ours** in the status filter. Old Network links still work.
- **What’s New and the Roadmap.** This page is new: what changed, newest first, with a mark in the header when there’s something you haven’t seen. The [Roadmap](/roadmap) shows what’s coming: now, next, and later.
- **Set a status on several listings at once.** In Network or Explore, click a listing’s photo to select it. Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows) adds one, Shift-click adds a range, and on a phone you press and hold. A bar at the bottom sets a status or Played Together for all of them, and you can Undo. [The shortcuts are on Help](/help#shortcuts).
- **Type of space for venues.** A venue can say what kind of place it is: dive bar, coffee shop, house show, record store, and more. Filter by it in Explore.
- **Invite a bandmate who’s already listed.** When you invite a bandmate, you can pick them from your band’s people instead of typing an email. They’re linked to that listing when they join, so nobody ends up listed twice.
- **Paste Members on the Add page.** Adding a band? Paste its lineup and pick who to add, roles and all.
- **No status on your own band.** Your band’s page no longer offers a status, plans, or outreach for itself.
- **Worn headings.** Big headings now look a little rubbed, like transfer lettering that’s been handled.
- **Fixes.** The loading metronome no longer hangs after a page has loaded, or after Show More. The phone menu scrolls when it’s taller than the screen. CSV exports list listings by type, then name. Signed in, About and the other info pages keep the usual header, without a Sign In button.

## 2026-11-10

- **Network and Explore.** My Network is now **Network**, and Full Directory is now **Explore**.
- **Better on phones.** The directory opens as a list. The header shows which page you’re on and switches pages, filters slide up from the bottom, and Add says what it does.
- **Getting Started, one step at a time.** New members get one thing to do at a time, starting with bands they might know.
- **Claim yourself.** When a band is invited, the invite can name your own profile too, so you claim your band and yourself in one go. Find Yourself also lists your band’s members.
- **CSV export keeps numbers as text.** Catalog numbers and phone numbers stay the way you typed them when you open an export in a spreadsheet.
- **Buttons show they’re working.** A button you pressed shows the metronome until it’s done.

## 2026-11-09

- **Step through listings in the quick view.** Press `J` and `K` for the next and previous listing, and `1` to `6` to set a status.
- **Edit more from the quick view.** Change a listing’s photo, town, and scenes without opening its page.
- **Switch between your bands.** In more than one band? Switch from the account menu.
- **Find yourself in any band’s directory.** If another band already added you, claim that listing instead of making a new one.
- **Town nicknames.** Scenes and town search know nicknames, like PDX for Portland.
- **Status headings stay put.** While you scroll the directory, the status you’re in stays pinned at the top.
- **Where Seeens Stands on AI.** [A plain page](/ai) on AI in Seeens: there isn’t any, and the rules if that ever changes.
